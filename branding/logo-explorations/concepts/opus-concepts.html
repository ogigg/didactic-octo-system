Sweaty — app icon concepts

Three original geometric marks built on tension, compression and release. Icon only: no wordmark, no letterforms, no illustration.

Ascent Ladder

One unbroken path steps upward in three equal gains, each tread lighter than the last, so the mark reads as measured progression carried across sessions.

Split Summit

A single disc is cleaved by two angled channels of pure negative space, the strata widening as they climb toward a resolved white peak.

Counterload

A heavy open ring holds the load while a lighter arc springs across its gap and releases into white, the geometry of stored effort turning into direction.
